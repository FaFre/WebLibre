This alpha adds tab locking and makes tapping a website's notification open the site. The Gecko engine moves from 159 to 160.

- Lock a tab so it cannot be closed by accident. Bulk closing and clearing skip locked tabs.
- Tapping a website's notification now opens the site in the right profile and container. Before, nothing opened.
- Private tabs get no notifications or push, so push subscriptions cannot outlive a private session.
- Switching profiles keeps push subscriptions, and cloned profiles and restored backups get their own.
- With Expert settings on: sleeping tabs, automatic sleep, and freezable containers that stop background activity.
- A locked profile no longer shows its pages while the app opens or in the app switcher.
- Android now asks you to confirm every WebLibre update.

If you sync settings, update all your devices to this alpha. If you use web push in more than one profile, some sites may need to subscribe again.

This is an alpha release. Back up important profiles before trying it.
