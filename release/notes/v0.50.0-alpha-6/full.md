A small alpha that tidies up the new built-in updater. It is also the first version that people on v0.50.0-alpha-5 can install straight from the App updates screen.

**This is an alpha release. Back up important profiles before trying it.**

## A clearer App updates screen

The App updates screen now looks like the rest of Settings, split into sections you can scan at a glance.

- **Status first.** The top section shows where things stand, the installed version and when WebLibre last checked. A coloured icon shows whether an update is waiting or something went wrong.
- **Clearer errors.** When something fails, the screen first says which step failed (checking, downloading, installing, or cancelling an installation), then gives the reason on a separate line.
- **What's new, one version at a time.** The newest version's notes are shown in full. If you skipped some versions, each one has its own row that you can tap to read its notes.
- **What changes** and **Settings** have their own sections. **Check for updates automatically** is under **Settings**. **Check now** sits in the status section when WebLibre is up to date, and under **Settings** otherwise.
- **Find it with settings search.** Searching Settings for **Check for updates automatically** or **Check now** takes you straight to the App updates screen.

## Everyday fixes and polish

- The update row at the top of the browser menu now looks like the other menu sections. Its coloured icon and dot still show when an update is waiting.
- The buttons in the update confirmation dialogs now look the same as in WebLibre's other confirmation dialogs.

## Upgrading from v0.50.0-alpha-5

There's nothing to install by hand: open **Settings > General > Updates > App updates** and tap **Check now**, or wait for the daily check.
