A small alpha that tidies up the built-in updater.

- The App updates screen now looks like the rest of Settings, with sections for status, what's new, what changes, and settings.
- Errors say which step failed, such as checking or downloading, and give the reason below.
- If you skipped versions, the notes for each one can be opened on their own.
- Settings search now finds Check for updates automatically and Check now.
- The update row in the browser menu matches the other menu sections.

This is an alpha release. Back up important profiles before trying it.
